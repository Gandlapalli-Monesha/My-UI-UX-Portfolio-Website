Blending Creativity

— A designer's origin story

In the bustling corridors of her college, Gandlapalli Monesha, a second-year B.Tech student studying Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, is steadily carving out her identity as a designer who doesn't fit neatly into any single box. Her portfolio spans UI/UX design, graphic design, web development, cybersecurity, and collaborative ventures.

Her involvement in six different student clubs — from a product development cell to sustainability forums — is testament to the truth that great design requires absorbing life from every direction. "Clubs are where ideas meet execution," she reflects.

Beyond the classroom, Monesha is a familiar face at hackathons and ideathons. For her, these events are more than competitions — they are laboratories of empathy. By building prototypes under deadline with diverse teams, she gains exposure to practical problems that require learning to adapt quickly.

"Design lets me narrate technology. It becomes a problem solver and a team player — the perfect intersection of creativity and technology."

Mobile UX · Onboarding

Microsoft Student Ambassadors, SRM: Mobile Onboarding Design

MSA ONBOARDING
GET STARTED

When the MSA Association SRM sought a mobile onboarding experience, the challenge matched the task with precision and purpose. The desktop website lacked the micro-interactions and emotional warmth that matched the experience. Using Lottie files and GIFs, she added subtle animations that made the experience feel alive and engaging.

02
Event Design · Web Redesign

Cherry's Community, SRM: Events Pages Redesign

CHERRY'S COMMUNITY
Workshop — Design Thinking
Talk — Community Building
Mixer — Creative Networking

For Cherry's Community, Monesha took on the challenge of redesigning the branding experience for their 2023 branding. She transformed dry event listings into immersive journeys. The redesign allows members to explore upcoming events and browse past sessions in a cinematic style — vivid event posters, richly colored stills, descriptions, and layered details that feel like a magazine editorial.

03
Luxury Brand · Landing Page

Prada: Landing Page Redesign

PRADA
NEW COLLECTION 2024
EXPLORE

Prada: a stock market analysis and consulting business inspired by the iconic fashion house. Monesha mapped the company's strategies and visual choices against the luxury brand ethos that conveyed professionalism while adding vibrancy. The result is an editorial landing page that speaks both to logic and elegance.

04
Author · Website Redesign

Suzanne Collins — Author Website Redesign

THE HUNGER GAMES
SUZANNE COLLINS
New York Times
Bestselling Author

When Monesha encountered Suzanne Collins' existing webpage, she noticed its lack of emotional appeal — a surprising gap for such a renowned author. She overhauled the site with clarity and cohesion, organizing content into well-structured sections that guided users seamlessly. She added collages of fan artwork, creating a vibrant showcase of the author's stories.

05
Tech Club · Website Redesign

CodeNex — Website Redesign

CODENEX
50+ Projects
10+ Awards
300+ Members
20+ Events
JOIN THE REVOLUTION →

The CodeNex website was a Bandhan Inspiration work with originality. Monesha restructured it with their unique brand identity. Her redesign emphasised clean hierarchy, striking tech aesthetics, and bold statistics front-and-center — reflecting the club's forward-thinking ethos. She integrated visuals from platforms like Vecteezy and Freepik, ensuring the digital presence felt both professional and grounded.

06
Personal App · Journal Design

Nocturne App — Personal Project

NOCTURNE
A JOURNAL APP

Nocturne is Monesha's personal exploration into journaling apps. It's a journal concept built with structured pages, encouraging introspection at its core. The app features guiding prompts and pages, arranged in layered layouts that encourage curiosity and deep reflection. Monesha stands as a designer who continues learning through iteration and building from the ground up.
