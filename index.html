— A designer's origin story
2021
Introduction to Design in Storytelling
Immersed in books, fandoms, and writing, I discovered how design could elevate storytelling. Platforms like Wattpad and AO3 revealed how visuals — character boards, trailers, and art — transform reading into a richer, more immersive experience. This was the spark that connected creativity with design for me.
2022
Early Design Practice with Canva
Back in offline school, I explored Canva for posters, timetables, and presentations. It became my playground for blending organization with design.
2023
Integrating Design into Creative Projects
As I began writing my own stories, I paired them with themed visuals, colors, fonts, and imagery to match their emotions. This was when I realized design wasn’t just decoration — it was part of the storytelling itself.
2024
Exploring Web Development vs. Design
Started my CSE journey and experimented with web development. While coding felt tedious, design sparked excitement. Pinterest moodboards and exposure to digital artists helped me realize my passion leaned toward design.
2025
The UI/UX Breakthrough
Through college clubs, I discovered UI/UX design. Recruitment tasks and collaborative projects introduced me to Figma and team workflows. I embraced UI/UX as the space where creativity meets usability.
2026
Combining Design and Development Skills
I took time to strengthen my development skills and build a stronger technical foundation. Now, I’m returning to design with a broader perspective — combining creativity and code to craft experiences that are both meaningful and usable.
